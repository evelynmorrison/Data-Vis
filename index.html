Untold stories of the “Happiest Countries”

Finland has topped the World Happiness Report for eight years running. But “happiness” there means one retrospective question — rate your life 0 to 10. Ask people how they actually feel day to day, and the picture rearranges itself. Click any country below to trace it across all four rankings.

Columns, left to right: World Happiness Report 2025, life evaluation rank (3-year average) · Wellbeing Rankings overall rank (Blanchflower & Bryson, 2024, Gallup World Poll microdata, re-ranked among 146 countries present in both sources) · Positive affect rank · Negative affect rank (rank 1 = least negative affect). Countries appearing in only one source were omitted; a handful of name variants (e.g. Czechia/Czech Republic, Türkiye/Turkey) were reconciled by hand. 146 countries shown of 164 in each source.
