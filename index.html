Untold stories of the “Happiest Countries”

Finland has topped the World Happiness Report for eight years running. But “happiness” there means one retrospective question — rate your life 0 to 10. Ask people how they actually felt yesterday and Taiwan comes first. Use a different index and the order changes again.

Happiness is slippery, subjective, and shaped by whoever defines it. How might we move toward a better more community informed measure?
